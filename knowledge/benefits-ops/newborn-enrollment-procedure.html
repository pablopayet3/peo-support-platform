Internal. For Benefits Ops. Northwind PEO is a fictional company; this procedure is part of a demo build.

When this applies

An employee (or a company admin acting for them) asks to add a newborn or an adopted child to their health plan. This is a qualifying life event: the enrollment window is 30 days from the event date, ending at 11:59 pm in the company's time zone. The ticket's due date is set to that deadline.

What is already done when the ticket reaches you

  • The AI intake (email agent, or the checks run when a person fills in the life-event fields) has filled Life event type, Event date and AI intake result.
  • The integration service has checked three things in the core platform: who is writing, whether there is an active plan, and whether the window is still open. The results are in an internal note and in the Northwind Benefits app in the ticket sidebar.
  • Zendesk has routed the ticket to Benefits Ops, as a task with the deadline as its due date. Failed checks are marked urgent.

Steps

  1. Open the Northwind Benefits app in the ticket sidebar. Check the life event, the deadline countdown and the three checks.
  2. If a check failed, follow the table below before anything else.
  3. Proof of birth. If the plan requires it to enroll, ask for it. A hospital record is enough when the plan accepts one; the birth certificate often takes weeks and can follow. If the plan does not require it, do not wait for it.
  4. Confirm the baby's legal name with the parent. It must match the proof of birth when there is one.
  5. Approve in the sidebar app. The date of birth must equal the Event date. The enrollment is created in the core platform as pending with the carrier; the app fills Enrollment outcome and Enrollment ID and adds an internal note. Never create the enrollment anywhere else.
  6. Reply to the customer that the enrollment is submitted, with the coverage start date (the date of birth) and any document still to come.
  7. Set the ticket to pending until the carrier confirms, then solve it.

When a check failed

AI intake resultWhat it meansWhat to do
Failed: identity not confirmedThe requester is not the employee, or is an admin of another company.Confirm who is writing. A company admin can act for an employee of the same company only. Never share plan details before identity is confirmed.
Failed: no active planThe employee has no active medical plan.Explain that a plan is needed first. If the employee believes this is wrong, check enrollment history in the core platform and escalate to Benefits Escalation.
Failed: outside enrollment windowMore than 30 days have passed since the event.Do not approve. If there is a documented reason for the delay, request a carrier exception through Benefits Escalation (supervisor approval). Otherwise set Enrollment outcome to Not eligible and explain the next open enrollment.
IncompleteThe checks could not run (date missing, company unclear, or a system was unavailable).Get the missing value, fill in the Event date, and the checks run again automatically.

Escalate

  • Less than 3 days before the deadline and not yet approvable: move to Benefits Escalation, priority urgent.
  • The sidebar app shows an error twice in a row: post the request ID from the internal note in #support-ops-alerts.
